Our story

Saltwood began with one hearth and one rule: if it doesn’t taste better over fire, it doesn’t make the menu. Everything we serve meets oak, ember or smoke, and comes from boats, farms and foragers within a hundred miles of our door.

MO

“Fire is the oldest seasoning there is. We just try to listen to it.”

Mara Okafor — Chef & Owner
